    You can submit a record/verification if you meet the following requirements:
    • The completion video must be uploaded to Youtube in decent quality.
    • The completion video must include the integrated single level timer and fps counter.
    • The completion video must include at least the last death or level entrance if done first try.
    • The completion video must include all game audio, music is optional.
    • Microphone audio is strongly recommended.
    • We may request additional information.
    • If a single campaign has more than 5 list-worthy levels, a maximum of 3 can be included at once.
    • We reserve the right to not approve a completion even if these parameters are met.
